BeamNG Engine & Transmission Swap Catalog - changelog
=====================================================

2.0  (BeamNG.drive 0.40.5)
-----
Engine models in vanilla cars (rebuilt from scratch):
- The donor engine's own model is placed on the car's gearbox (its crank on the car's gearbox
  axis) instead of only on the block; the fit check also checks the turbo / intake parts.
- New "Engine model" slot per swap: Donor engine model / Car's own engine model / No engine model.
- New tuning sliders (visual only): move left/right, forward/back, up/down, turn, and flip the
  gearbox end for engines turned 90 degrees.
- Turbo kits and intakes picked in the tuning slots now show on the swapped engine (they used
  to show only in the engine's own car). Front-mounted intercoolers stay hidden.
- Native engines (the car's own engine options) keep their full factory look and exhaust.
- The car's exhaust manifold / exhaust slots move under the catalog engine, so the right
  headers show for each engine.
Generated parts (new):
- Bellhousing adapters: wherever the swapped engine does not meet the car's gearbox, a part is
  made for that engine and car, shaped by the gearbox mouth and the back of the engine (exact
  surface contact), with flanges, bolts and ribs. Engines moved up to 15 cm by the fit check
  get an offset bellhousing.
- Bent exhaust pipes from the donor's header outlet to the car's exhaust (up to 80 cm, mandrel
  bends at least 2x the pipe radius, at least 1 cm from the body, frame and suspension), sized
  from both pipes, with a flange + bolts and a clamp.
- Optional Blender pass (bevelled edges, smooth shading, triangle limits); models are written
  into each car's own folder so every car only loads its own.
- Materials: cast aluminium / dark cast iron for adapters, exhaust steel for pipes (or the
  car's own gearbox colour when it has no texture).
Tool:
- The window and "py -m swapcat build" build all this by default; Blender path box added.
- The validator also checks every generated model (it loads, triangle limits, materials,
  node groups, visibility switch) - 0 errors in all 47 cars.
- Report: per car and engine, the joint grade (perfect / adapter / too far / ...), the exhaust
  result and the reason when nothing was added.

1.1  (not released on its own; included in 2.0)

  tolerance, "hide engines that don't fit" and "(clips)" options.

1.0  (BeamNG.drive 0.40.5)
-----
